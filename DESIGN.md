# Design

The store is a press reading room in the house colors, not a generic shop skin.

## Color

Light paper is sand `#f6efe1`, cards `#fffaf0`, ink `#1b1712`, gold `#8a5a14` / `#e2b866`. Dark paper is `#12100c` with gold `#e2b866` as the accent. Chapter heads (home hero and inner page titles) stay ink in both themes so the gold rule reads the same. Buttons that sit on gold use ink text.

## Type

Page and section titles use Aref Ruqaa. Interface labels use Noto Kufi Arabic. Body copy uses IBM Plex Sans Arabic. Prices are tabular. Titles are not gradient-filled.

## Signature

A short gold rule draws under a page title or section title (scale from the start edge). It is already at full length when reduced motion is requested. Every cover sits in the same paper plate: aspect 4/5, a gold hairline, and the full photograph contained so titles are not cropped. Portrait jackets fill the plate; wider photographs keep even paper margins. The plate stays paper in dark mode so a dark jacket still reads.

The home hero keeps the newest book on a light gold stage, separate from the ink slideshow, so the jacket color does not have to match the night background.

## Chrome

A 3px gold filament caps the header. The wordmark is Ruqaa. Desktop navigation is a text index with a gold underline for the current page. Mobile uses a solid bottom bar with a gold top edge, not a glass strip. The footer repeats email and WhatsApp `971563244942`.

## Catalog

From 1024px up, search, category, sort, stock, and price sit in one bench. Below that they live in a drawer opened by «تصفية وترتيب», closed with Escape or «عرض النتائج». Active filters stay visible as chips outside the drawer. Shipping copy stays 25 AED.

## Book

Gallery, then facts. The price is a plate. Buy and quantity stick to the bottom once the main buttons leave the viewport, above the mobile nav, and the back-to-top control sits above that bar. Checkout remains a Shopify permalink.

## Motion

Page enter, the gold rule, drawer slide, and the existing cover tilt. Nothing loops except the interior slideshow, which can be paused, and which stops under reduced motion.
